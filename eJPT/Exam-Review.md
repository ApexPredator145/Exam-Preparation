## Introduction

I recently passed the eJPT exam and earned the eJPT certificate. eJPT is a professional certification exam from INE. It is a beginner-level certification which costs around ₹21,040 / $249 USD for the exam + official training course by Alexis Ahamad for 3 months. The exam voucher must be used within 180 days, it will expire if unused. The exam has 48 hours to solve and it consists of a practical penetration testing scenario. The exam is 100% hands-on lab.

## Who am I?

My name is Aaditya Manoj Oza, I have been studying cybersecurity for more than one year. I am eJPT certified and have earned SEC0 and SEC1 certifications from TryHackMe as well. I am currently ranked in top 4000 globally on TryHackMe by solving hundreds of CTFs and completing near 400 labs. 

## Why I chose eJPT?

Although I had SEC0 and SEC1 certifications from TryHackMe, my ultimate goal is OSCP right now and eJPT is considered as stepping stone toward advanced certifications such as OSCP. Therefore, I decided to buy the eJPT voucher and attempt the exam. 

## Preparation of eJPT

As I said before, I have been learning cybersecurity for more than a year now so I was not a complete beginner. I followed and completed half of the official training course by Alexis Ahamad and the course is amazing for beginners; however, I felt most of the concepts were already known to me; therefore, I decided to prepare by practicing more rather than watching the course content. 

### My Preparation

I started my practical preparation by first solving the official SkillCheck labs from INE which are part of the course itself, moving forward I continued with easy/medium challenges on TryHackMe as well as all the beginner path boxes in Hack The Box. 

### Recommended Preparation

The official training by Alexis Ahamad is definitely enough to pass the exam, but I would suggest more practical preparation rather than just watching the course content and only practicing on the SkillCheck labs. You can check out my GitHub repo which has a list of all the recommended boxes across platforms like TryHackMe, Hack The Box, etc. for eJPT.

## My Exam Experience 

After preparing for quite a time, I decided to finally attempt the exam. One tip I want to share is when you are able to solve boxes on your own and you understand what you are exactly doing, just attempt the exam and do not fall into continuous loop of preparation. You will never feel ready for the exam completely and always want more practice.
I started the exam in the morning around 10 AM, I had decided to not panic even if I get stuck and I began slowly by documenting every step. I followed my methodology and did not try to perform random enumeration. The exam questions themselves guide you in exam, this is one of the reasons it is beginner level certification as the questions explicitly hints you what you need to do in the exam. I finished the exam around 11 PM in night including the breaks, it took me 12 hours because my exam instance glitched and I was not able to get answers of few questions due to the glitch, even one of the flags was missing completely. I mailed the INE support regarding my issue and got response very quickly, but the support claimed there is no glitch and the exam instances are running fine.. by the night I was frustrated and even decided to submit the exam without answering the remaining questions but then I decided to give it one final try and also changed my lab region. After changing the region, my glitch was resolved and I was able to find the remaining flag and answers to questions, I quickly completed the remaining tasks and submitted the exam. I got the "You passed" screen with 88% of score, I wanted more than 90% but what I got isn't bad score either.. I was surprised to get 88% because I answered every question and my answers seemed right to me, but I was satisfied with the results and was finally relieved by earning the certificate.
I received another response from INE next morning containing an apology for the exam glitch which confirmed the glitch was from their side. I don't want to say INE has bad infra, glitches do happen, INE's support was top-notch and I got the responses for my queries pretty quickly. Apart from the glitch the latency and connections were smooth and perfect.

## Should you try eJPT? 

If you are a beginner then I highly recommend to go for eJPT if you can, this is because the exam is 100% hands-on practical, practical skills are very very important and specially when you are new in the field you need to build and test your practical skills not just theoretical knowledge. Other certifications like CompTIA Security+ are recognized and good for bypassing the HR filters but they are not practical and cybersecurity is not a theory only field.. practical knowledge is must have for cybersecurity if you really want to grow. 

The training course is also great for beginners as it teaches from fundamentals. If you are not a complete beginner but intermediate like me, I would still recommend eJPT as your first certification if you don't have any other prior certifications, since eJPT simulates real world penetration testing scenario, it cements your methodology and also boosts your confidence level once you pass the exam. It is great stepping stone for advanced certifications such as OSCP.

## Honest Review

I liked the exam and the training. The exam is not completely objective based and you can't just predict the answers. I will not say it is the best certification available on the planet, but for beginners it is one of the best available. Specially because it is practical focused, it evaluates your practical skills and not just theoretical knowledge.

There are certain topics I am disappointed about in exam, including pivoting and privilege escalation. I do not want to disclose anything sensitive but I was disappointed with the pivoting section in the exam as I expected more from it.
For me, the training course also felt repetitive on certain topics, though because I am not a complete beginner, this can be my issue only as I knew most of the concepts already.

## My Advice/Tips

The biggest tip is do not panic, the exam is not CTF style where you will have to think out of the box, the exam rewards good enumeration. Just perform good enumeration, try to chain the findings, and do not stick to only one wordlist or tool, use the tools and wordlists according to the situation. The exam is beginner-friendly and if you follow your methodology and perform strong enumeration then you can pass the exam with good score!
