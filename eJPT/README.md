# eJPT

This guide contains detailed guidance on preparing for the eJPT exam including suggested rooms to practice, resources to use for learning and the required skills you must acquire before starting the exam.

<br>

## What is eJPT ?

eJPT is a beginner-level certification exam from INE. The exam has a 48-hour time limit where you have to perform a penetration test on the given IP range. The exam is 100% hands-on practical and there is no theory in the exam. The exam tests your practical skills, methodology and problem-solving for performing the penetration test.
eJPT costs around $249 USD/₹21,000 INR. The prices may change and vary as per your region.

You can read my detailed review of the exam [here](eJPT/Exam-Review.md).

## Recommended Labs & Challenges 

I have listed some challenges across platforms including TryHackMe and HackTheBox. I myself have solved these challenges and can say that these challenges are great for the exam and test your skills in various categories.

Another resource which I did not list here but I used most was AI, I did not use AI to solve challenges or write code, but I used it as learning resource. Whenever you are learning something and you feel that you cannot understand it at all then you should use AI. Use any model you want, be it ChatGPT, be it Claude but use it for learning and not for solving challenges. Prompt the model to teach you the particular topic in detail from basic and you can continue asking questions on part you are confused until everything is cleared! This is my best method to learn, I try to learn from other sources first and if I don't understand any topic then I use AI to clear my doubts and it works like magic!
